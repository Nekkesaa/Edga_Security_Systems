02 About

About Edga

Trusted security for homes, businesses and events across Kenya.

Edga Security Systems is a registered Kenyan security company, established in August 2015. We provide trained guards, alarms, CCTV, access control and electric fencing, with each solution built around the client's needs.

Our Mission
To answer the security concerns of businesses and individuals with disciplined, well-trained guards backed by modern technology, at competitive prices.
Our Vision
Cost-effective security that never sacrifices quality, with services that are simple, transparent and within reach of every client.
Our Values
Professionalism, customer focus, teamwork and continuous improvement.
